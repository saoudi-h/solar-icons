Solar Icons — browse and insert 1,451 icons in six styles (Bold, BoldDuotone, Broken, Linear, LineDuotone, Outline) directly in Figma.

This is the maintained distribution of the Solar icon set: standardized SVGs, corrected names, editable 1.5 px strokes for Linear, Broken, and LineDuotone, live stroke-width preview, fuzzy search, and dark/light themes. What you insert matches the published Solar Icons packages.
