## 2.0.0

* First stable release, aligned with the Solar Icons v2 package family.
* 1,451 icons in six styles (Bold, BoldDuotone, Broken, Linear, LineDuotone, Outline) as generated widgets, plus a style-switching `SolarIcon` widget, `SolarTheme` defaults, and deprecated-name typedefs.
